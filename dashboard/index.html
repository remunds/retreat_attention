Retreat Attention experiments

Seaquest (current): agents are trained on the base game only. The held-out test is the gravity mod, which slowly pulls the submarine down. The goal is to collect 6 divers and surface with them at least twice per game there, averaged over at least 10 games.

Pong (done): world model and actor trained on unmodified Pong only; held-out test lazy_enemy (the enemy paddle only follows the ball while it approaches); goal: final score of at least +10.

Built by dashboard/build_dashboard.py from the experiment files, their Markdown notes and runs/. To add an experiment, create experiment_x.py with experiment_x.md and rebuild: CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES= uv run dashboard/build_dashboard.py.
Videos use the newest checkpoint of each experiment's most recent run. Pong: the actor in the real lazy_enemy game, and the actor inside its own world model started from real Pong or real lazy_enemy histories; lines connect each object to the objects its next-step prediction uses (opacity = gate value). Seaquest: the agent in the real base game and in the held-out gravity game, from the same seed.
