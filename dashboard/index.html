Object-centric Pong world model · JAXAtari

Retreat Attention experiments

Every experiment trains a world model and an actor on unmodified Pong only. The held-out test is the lazy_enemy mod, where the enemy paddle only follows the ball while the ball moves towards it. The goal is a final score (agent points minus enemy points) of at least +10 there, averaged over at least 10 games.

Built by dashboard/build_dashboard.py from the experiment files, their Markdown notes and runs/. To add an experiment, create experiment_x.py with experiment_x.md and rebuild: CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES= uv run dashboard/build_dashboard.py.
Videos: the newest checkpoint of each experiment's most recent run, played inside its own world model (imagination), started from real Pong histories. Lines connect each object to the objects its next-step prediction uses; opacity is the gate value.
